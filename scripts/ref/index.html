Examination Rules

Ensure exam integrity by following these guidelines carefully

Login Security

Use only your assigned registration number and access code. Never share your credentials with anyone.

Browser Restrictions

Do not switch tabs or windows during the exam. The system auto-submits if the tab is changed.

Copy/Paste Disabled

Copying and pasting is fully disabled during examinations to maintain academic integrity.

Timed Examination

All exams are strictly timed. Auto-submission occurs automatically when time expires.
