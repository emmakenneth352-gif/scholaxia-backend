Scholaxia helps Nigerian students prepare for JAMB, WAEC, NECO, Post-UTME, and school exams with CBT practice, live classes, study materials, and AI tutoring.

We combine real teachers, an AI tutor, and computer-based test practice so every student can revise smarter and walk into exam day ready.
